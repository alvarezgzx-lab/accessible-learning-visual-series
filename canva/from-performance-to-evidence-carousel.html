Publication 1 · Beyond interaction

From Performance
to Evidence

The Human-Centred Digital Learning Loop

An activity is not evidence of performance simply because it is interactive.

A nine-part evidence-informed carousel for LXD and L&D practice.

01 / 09

The bridge from Publication 0

The stack expands what L&D can build.

It does not decide what deserves to be built.

Expanded capability

AI-assisted development, UX/UI, JavaScript or JSX, and xAPI can broaden prototyping, personalization and instrumentation.

Human judgement

L&D still defines the performance, the evidence, the criteria and the limits of interpretation.

The opportunity is not “more screens.” It is greater continuity between analysis, design, construction and measurement.

Human-centred systems should preserve stakeholder agency and contextual relevance (Alfredo et al., 2024; Buckingham Shum et al., 2024).

02 / 09

The complete view

The Human-Centred Digital Learning Loop

1Target Performance

Define what a person must accomplish in context.

2Evidence & Criteria

Specify what observable output could support the claim.

3Authentic Practice

Recreate the decisions and constraints that matter.

4Human-Centred Experience

Make the task understandable, controllable and accessible.

5Meaningful Events

Record actions only when they relate to the evidence model.

6Interpret & Iterate

Use bounded interpretations to improve the design.

This loop is a design synthesis for this series—not a validated universal framework. It integrates authentic assessment, ECD, human-centred LA and closed-loop LA (Alfredo et al., 2024; Gholson et al., 2024; Sailer et al., 2024; Zhan et al., 2025).

01–02 · Start with the claim

Performance first. Evidence before activity.

Claim

What capability or performance do we expect?

→

Evidence

What observable product, decision or explanation would support that claim?

→

Task

What situation can elicit that evidence under meaningful conditions?

Evidence-Centered Design links claims, evidence and tasks. In workplace learning, the translation still requires domain validation.

Evidence-Centered Design connects claims, evidence and tasks (Gholson et al., 2024). Complete APA 7 reference on slide 9.

03 · Authentic practice

Recreate meaningful decisions—not screen gestures.

Practice should preserve

Relevant decisions, constraints, criteria, feedback and consequences.

Authenticity is multidimensional

It may involve task, context, cognitive challenge, criteria, agency and collaboration—not realism alone.

Design test

  • Does the learner make the kind of decision the role requires?
  • Can the output be judged against explicit criteria?
  • Could the task support transfer, or does it only reward interface navigation?

Zhan, Y., Boud, D., & Du, Z. (2025). Designing for authentic assessment: A scoping review. Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10734-025-01588-9

04 · Human-centred experience

Accessibility and agency are part of the learning architecture.

HCLA

Engage affected people, preserve agency and evaluate systems in context.

UDL 3.0

Offer multiple representations, accessible action and meaningful options for engagement.

WCAG 2.2

Support perception, keyboard operation, understandable interaction and robust semantics.

Human-centred does not mean “pleasant-looking.” It means designing with and for the people affected by the system.

Alfredo et al. (2024), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2024.100215 · Buckingham Shum et al. (2024), https://doi.org/10.1111/bjet.13442 · CAST (2024) · W3C (2023).

05 · Meaningful events

What gets recorded is not automatically what was learned.

Activity→

The designed opportunity to practise.

Interaction→

A learner action within the interface.

Event→

A structured record of an action or state.

Evidence→

An observable output relevant to a defined claim.

Interpretation→

A bounded inference based on evidence, criteria and context.

xAPI can structure events. It cannot determine whether those events constitute valid evidence of capability.

Closed-loop learning analytics emphasizes theory-informed links between data collection, analysis, intervention and evaluation (Sailer et al., 2024). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2024.108305

07 / 09

06 · Interpret, transfer & iterate

The stack supports traceability. L&D defines meaning.

UX/UI

Presents choices, criteria, feedback and consequences clearly.

JavaScript / JSX

Implements states, branching, feedback and consequences.

xAPI / events

Records selected actions and context using a shared event vocabulary.

L&D judgement

Interprets evidence, tests assumptions and decides what to improve.

Technology makes the experience observable. It does not make every observation meaningful.

Closed-loop learning analytics links collection, analysis, intervention and evaluation (Sailer et al., 2024). Complete APA 7 reference on slide 9.

Implications · limits · references

Upskilling creates an opportunity—not a guarantee.

Technical upskilling may create greater continuity across analysis, design, construction and measurement—and more options for teams with constrained software budgets.

Hypothesis—not demonstrated impact: effectiveness, workplace transfer, savings, speed and superiority remain unproven.
